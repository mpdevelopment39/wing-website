2. What stays on your device

Sound identification runs on your device using a machine learning model. Wing does not send recordings to an AI service to identify them. Suggestions are informational and can be wrong.

Your journal, notes, attached photos and recordings are stored locally. No Wing account is required. If you export a backup or share content, you choose the destination; Files, iCloud and other apps apply their own terms. Original photos may contain location metadata. Review what you share.

4. When Wing connects to the Internet

Birdsong analysis runs locally. Other features may require a connection:

  • Nearby birds: requests to GBIF include the selected geographic area, which may be derived from your location, and date filters. Your journal and recordings are not included in these queries.
  • Maps and species pages: Apple MapKit and providers of images, reference sounds or species information (such as Wikimedia and GBIF) receive the requests needed to deliver content, including the IP address and requested resource. Individual content credits appear in the app.
  • Subscriptions: Apple and RevenueCat receive the data needed to validate purchases, provide your storefront price and restore access. Apple subscription events are synchronized with RevenueCat.

We do not integrate advertising or cross-app advertising tracking. The version described does not include a behavioral analytics SDK. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Providers and international transfers

We use providers to deliver these features. Apple operates App Store, payments and system services; RevenueCat validates subscriptions and manages their status. GBIF and content providers respond to the queries you initiate. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where applicable, transfers must rely on adequacy decisions or contractual safeguards, including standard contractual clauses. You can request information about relevant safeguards through our privacy contact.

Read more: Apple privacy, RevenueCat privacy, GBIF privacy and Wikimedia privacy.

6. Permissions and trial reminder

You can change permissions in iOS Settings. If you authorize notifications and have an active eligible free trial expected to renew, Wing schedules a local reminder for 48 hours before it ends. This does not require sending your recordings or using an advertising notification provider.

Delivery depends on iOS and your settings. Uninstalling the app, disabling notifications or system restrictions may prevent delivery. Wing updates or removes the reminder when it synchronizes subscription status; a change outside the app may not appear until you open it again. The reminder does not cancel your subscription. Manage it in Apple Settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Local content remains until you delete it or uninstall the app, subject to backups you have created through iOS, Files or iCloud. Trash and exported backups must be managed separately. Deleting Wing does not cancel a subscription or delete purchase records Apple must retain.

Subscription and support records are kept as needed to manage access, resolve issues and meet legal obligations or claims periods. Providers apply their own retention rules to data they control. You can request deletion of data we control, subject to legal exceptions. We cannot promise to delete records Apple is required to retain.

8. Your rights

Where the law provides, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, including objection to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier processing. Contact info@legal-assistant-ai.com; we may ask for the minimum information needed to verify your identity or locate a purchase identifier. Never send passwords or payment card details.

You may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or the competent authority where you live. We do not use bird identification to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

9. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added advertising cookies, visitor analytics, forms or remotely hosted fonts. GitHub may log IP addresses and technical data for security and hosting under its privacy statement. Links to other websites are governed by their operators’ policies. If you email us, we receive the information you include.

10. Children and updates

Wing is not specifically directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly request their personal data. A legal representative must be involved when parental consent or authorization is required by applicable law. If you believe we have received a child’s data without the necessary authorization, contact us so we can investigate.

We will update this policy when features or data practices change. The revision date appears at the top. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate notices in the app or on this website.
